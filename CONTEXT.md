# Vidra product contract

Current product decisions for anyone changing the code. Engineering rules live in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md). Before UI work, read the relevant [design docs](#design-docs); the Figma file (`figma://file/hVtzoSXhV2rQmxc6pOYC8c/Untitled`) is the source for current designs.

## Direction

Vidra helps creators make and edit images, video clips and finished videos, including ads. All three are intended outputs: an image does not have to become a video, and a video does not have to start with a generated image.

There is no required creation order ([ADR-0024](docs/adr/0024-creation-has-no-required-order.md)). A creator can start with a description, an image, a sketch or existing footage, then choose what to make or change. Expanding a description, generating a starting image and animating it are optional actions, never prerequisites.

The creator can tell what an action will do, which media it uses and where the result will go. Viewing another result never changes an unfinished request, and a completed generation never takes over work started while it ran. Making, saving, selecting, editing and exporting media are separate facts; the workspace does not collapse them into one stage. The outcome is usable work the creator can export and return to; a successful provider response alone does not prove the result meets the request. Authoring assistance is a useful capability; its competitive advantage must be demonstrated rather than declared. The exact workspace design, buyer positioning and pricing are not settled by this direction.

Testing is free under [ADR-0023](docs/adr/0023-bounded-free-validation-proposal.md). The Studio daily spend cap, which bounds every native request, and the Sketch bounds remain. Paid launch, new provider spending and background schedules need separate approval.

## Workspace

The project/conversation/draft workspace implements creation, phrase editing, history/versions, imports, Media, Sketch, video editing/export, sharing, project management, auth/account and public/help. New requests dispatch the selected qualified action directly, with explicit input roles and immutable settings/destination capture.

- **Library.** Signed in, `/app` opens the owner's library on its conversations with a fresh composer and no project selected ([ADR-0026](docs/adr/0026-work-starts-in-an-owner-library-projects-are-folders.md)). The library reuses its newest unused conversation or starts one; once something is made, the URL moves to that conversation. Media and video edits are library-wide.
- **Projects are folders** at `/app/projects`, listed beside earlier projects. Opening one shows its name, its conversations and a composer that files new conversations into it; project options rename or trash it. Library conversations offer Add to project and Remove from project. Earlier projects open at `/app/project/:id`.
- **Signed out**, `/app` shows the same create workspace: visitors can write, choose an action and a model; phrase highlighting and suggestions are off. Make, Improve wording, Reference or Sketch keeps the words on the device and opens sign-up with a claim. After sign-in the matching draft moves into a new conversation and the writer presses Make again. A missing or mismatched claim imports nothing. Signed-out project links show the access page. Evidence: `folder.integration.test.ts`, the library cases in `project.integration.test.ts`, `useLibraryEntry.test.ts` and `GuestWorkspace.test.tsx`.
- **Retired `/session`.** The legacy workspace was retired on 2026-10-19: `/session/*`, `/prompt/:uuid` and `/history` redirect to `/app`; stored sessions are neither migrated nor deleted.
- **Retired Studio and Live editor.** Both retired into `/app` on 2026-10-22 ([ADR-0027](docs/adr/0027-studio-and-the-live-editor-retire-into-app.md)): `/studio`, `/studio/*` and `/live-editor` redirect to `/app`, and their **Use in a video** and **Use this** bridges are gone. Pictures they already sent are ordinary library imports. Studio's models are workflow profiles, its Auto becomes the model chip's Auto, and Sketch in `/app` covers the Live editor. Studio projects and turns are neither migrated nor deleted. Their owner can still list, open and delete them through `GET`/`DELETE /api/studio/projects…`, and reading a turn that a dead process left running releases its unspent cents.
- **Media** (`?view=media`) is a picture/clip grid with search, type and sort, non-destructive hide/restore, exact-version viewing and copies to another owned project. Imports are fingerprint-bound resumable uploads; the server verifies content, geometry and duration before publishing a media version. Dismissing an import leaves it running; cancelling stops it. An import never restores a removed input or takes over another draft.
- **Leaving work.** The router blocks unsafe departures, including browser Back and conversation-query changes, with the leave dialog. Creating another draft never deactivates work without a safe copy.

## Composer

- **Layout (desktop).** The generations feed is the stage: the App rail, a 72-wide history rail (one item per request, newest last; hover shows a quick look), a 720-wide feed of request blocks (words and setup line, results at feed size, their verbs as buttons) and the wide composer docked under it, growing upward. Focus opens one result large on demand over the workspace, with the history rail as its filmstrip; closing it keeps the feed's scroll. Mobile keeps the conversation panel and viewer until its mirrors are designed.
- **One composer.** A **Video · Image · Voiceover · Music** switch beside the primary picks what to make, Video first. Upscale, Reframe and Captions are rail tools after Sketch; a tool opens the same composer in place with its input and settings and hides the switch. Actions come from the shared registry (`shared/workflow/actionRegistry.ts`): each names its label, place, words policy and core chips, and a new action is one entry plus its profiles. Improve wording and Ask a question sit at the right of the input row, are disabled while the words are empty, and are absent for an exact voiceover script.
- **Words follow the profile:** required, optional guidance (Reframe), an exact script without highlighting (voiceover) or none (Upscale, Captions).
- **Results show their verbs as buttons** (Rule 8): under each result in the feed, in the library's Media action bar for the selected card, and in Focus's Use menu. They come from the qualified profiles that take the result's kind — picture: New variation, Edit, Edit a region, Animate, Upscale, Use as reference; clip: Edit, Extend, Upscale, Reframe, Make captions, Use a frame, Add to video; audio: Add to video, Make captions — and each opens the same composer with the result bound. Audio takes are feed rows with play, waveform and length; captions are a text card with Add to video and Download .vtt. Media and the picker filter All, Pictures, Clips and Audio.
- **Inputs.** The input row offers every role the place's models take (Picture, Start frame, End frame, Reference, Clip, Audio); a clip arrives on Video only from a result's Edit or Extend. **Inputs decide the model:** attaching or removing one moves the request to the selected model's own version that takes what is attached (a start frame turns Wan 3 into Wan 3 image-to-video), else to the place's first model that does. On a switch, each input moves to the role the new place uses for its kind; one it cannot use stays in the row as a greyed Not used tile, out of the request, and comes back on switching back. The model menu starts with **Auto**, keeps one entry per model and greys out, without a reason, every model that cannot take the attached inputs. Auto's chips offer what its candidates offer; Make resolves Auto to one exact model, which the request records.
- Prompt highlights are automatic when available; there is no off switch. Choosing a replacement applies it immediately after current-draft and exact-target validation. If the target no longer resolves, the creator selects the phrase again.

## Capabilities

- There is one capability system: workflow capability profiles in `shared/schemas/workflow/capability.schemas.ts`, offered by `server/src/services/creation-requests/preparation/WorkflowCapabilityCatalog.ts`. Each profile states its parameters as editable, fixed, derived, unsupported or unknown, plus input roles, generated sound and cross-setting constraints. The composer reads only these, so a new model needs only a profile.
- Each action has a fixed core chip row (Rule 6 of the research): picture — model, shape, resolution, count; clip — model, shape, duration, resolution, sound, count; voiceover — model, voice, count; music — model, duration, count; Captions — model, language; Upscale — model, scale; Reframe — model, shape. A core setting the selected model cannot use stays in place as a greyed chip with no chevron and does not open. A menu lists the values other models of the same place offer; values this model or its constraints cannot use are greyed and cannot be chosen. No text explains why. Profiles declare each setting's role, label, value names and number format (`presentation`); the client never guesses a role from a parameter name. Every other setting sits under one **Advanced** chip, last, as a menu, labelled slider, toggle or text field; seeds stay out (New variation and Reuse setup decide them). Choosing another model keeps the values it can use, moves the rest to its nearest offer (by number for seconds and resolutions, otherwise its default) and drops settings it does not have. A value that constrains another setting moves that setting too (1080p moves duration to 8s). A restored setup is not rewritten and an incompatibility there stays visible; the red Invalid outline is only for a restored model that is no longer offered.
- Twelve exact native Replicate profiles, each offering every documented setting and reserving its maximum from the Studio daily cap, live in `server/src/services/creation-requests/replicate-models/`: Wan 3, Gemini Omni 1.1, Grok Imagine Extend, Topaz Video Upscale, Luma Reframe, Nano Banana 2.1, GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst, Ideogram 4.5 Precise Edit, Topaz Upscale, ElevenLabs v3, Lyria 3 and Scribe v2. They add the actions `upscale-video`, `reframe-video`, `make-voiceover`, `make-music` and `transcribe`, an `audio` output kind and a free-text setting type. Studio's former roster adds fourteen more in `studioPictureModels.ts`, listed cheapest first within each action: Recraft V4.1, V4.1 Pro and their Vector versions, Nano Banana 2, 2 Lite and Pro, and GPT Image 2 for making (and, except Recraft, editing), plus Recraft Remove Background and Recraft Vectorize. Those last two serve the picture actions `remove-background` and `vectorize-image`, which are reached only from a picture's buttons. The three SVG profiles are listed as unavailable until workflow output can store and verify SVG. The composer reaches all of them; the [model capabilities Figma log](docs/design/model-dependent/replicate-models-figma-log.md) records their designs.
- Generation offers come from `shared/videoModels.ts`. Historical model/provider ids remain readable; prompt compilation keeps separate targets. Retiring an adapter does not rewrite saved takes.
- Area drawing is implemented. End-frame, reference-video, extension and area generation stay unavailable for a model without a qualified adapter, and unsupported modes fail closed. Mask drawing is tied to exact source geometry and never falls back to a whole-picture request.

## Spending

Native dispatch consumes accepted immutable request slots, and media is linked only after owned bytes are stored and verified. Per-slot provider reservations consume the Studio daily cap atomically; a repeated request cannot spend or dispatch twice. The default media cost policy pins complete adapter mappings and expires on November 17, 2026; new, changed or expired policies fail closed.

Gemini Omni takes no duration, so its ceilings assume an 11-second clip. A verified clip over 11 seconds is still delivered, but its profile is then held on every instance through a `workflow_spend_holds` record. API servers load holds at startup and every five minutes; the model list marks a held or expired profile unavailable, and preparation blocks its drafts before acceptance. A held profile re-opens only after its ceiling is reviewed, the record is deleted and the servers restart. Native text actions need a reviewed exact provider/model/context/output-token ceiling; no unverified text price is enabled. Operators may set a validated `WORKFLOW_SPEND_POLICY_JSON` (`WorkflowSpendPolicySchema`); that does not authorize provider calls during development.

## History and state

| Term                | Meaning                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Library             | The owner's home for work outside a named project. One per owner, created on first use; it cannot be renamed or trashed.                           |
| Project             | A named folder that library conversations are filed into, and back out of, without moving their work. Earlier projects keep their own history.     |
| Guest draft         | Words, action and settings written while signed out, kept in that browser for a day and moved into the library after sign-in. Never submitted.     |
| Conversation        | Chronological accepted requests, questions, answers and results. Editing an old request appends a revision; it does not erase later work.          |
| Draft               | Independently saved unfinished instructions, action, explicit inputs, settings, context and destination. A project can retain more than one.       |
| Request             | An immutable accepted snapshot of one submitted action, with its receipt and fixed output slots.                                                   |
| Media version       | One immutable image/clip/file with exact source versions and producing request. An edit creates a child; another attempt uses the original inputs. |
| Video edit revision | One saved arrangement of specific media versions, timing, text and audio.                                                                          |
| Export              | A stored file produced from one captured video edit revision. Later editing does not change it.                                                    |

Looking at a version does not select it as an input. A new request uses explicit branch context rather than all later messages. Undo changes unfinished text or video editing; it never cancels generation or reverses spending. Background completion can resolve its own explicitly waiting draft input but never replaces unrelated work. Ready imported media can enable creation while the input keeps its exact import receipt. New variation clears an inherited seed; **Reuse setup** keeps it. The [workflow state contract](docs/design/workflow/workflow-state-contract.md) and [ADR-0025](docs/adr/0025-conversations-requests-and-media-versions-have-separate-history.md) specify the detailed transitions, conflicts and recovery; they are implementation targets, not descriptions of shipped behavior.

Finished-video assembly, trimming, caption/audio editing and immutable export are implemented. An edit has a voiceover lane between clip sound and music; a take is placed only by an explicit Add to video (a voiceover take to Voiceover, other audio to Music, never over an occupied lane), and a transcript's Add to video places its cues as captions in one change. Video edits persist immutable revisions and compare-and-set operation receipts. Native FFmpeg renders one saved revision, verifies streams, frame count and hash, and stores an immutable export media version; editing can continue meanwhile. Lost delivery repairs retained bytes; an unknown render outcome never rerenders automatically. Derived previews and frame extraction pin the source object generation, and cache cleanup never deletes originals.

Exact-version public links use `/share/media/:id`, a revocation-checked same-origin stream with byte ranges; legacy `/share/:uuid` links keep their reader. Public snapshots omit prompts, settings and conversation data. Downloads use attachment grants. Support opens an email handoff or manual copy and never claims a message was sent or data deleted.

Dispatch captures words, inputs, model, settings and destination. Receipts recover accepted artifacts after lost responses. Video jobs keep claims, leases and terminal failure evidence. Durable completion precedes attachment, and repair reuses the stored media, take id, session and words version without resubmission or refunds. Uploaded references are owned durable copies with explicit words association. Selection inspects artifacts without changing the working draft, tool panels persist across context changes, and playback requires a selected playable clip. Generic sessions and saved camera/model/continuity fields stay readable; retiring source never migrates or deletes stored records.

## Retired — do not rebuild

- The legacy capability registry, `/api/capabilities` (with `/models`, `/providers` and `/registry`), its probe service and `sync:capabilities`.
- Studio as a place ([ADR-0027](docs/adr/0027-studio-and-the-live-editor-retire-into-app.md)): its route, thread, plane and project index; its model registry and Auto routing; its six-way turn policy and its turn, roster, pin and attachment routes. Also the standalone Live editor page and both bridges into the library.
- Named assets/triggers, depth/convergence, continuity generation, storyboards/character preprocessing, recommendation, coherence/observation endpoints, paid intake and broad automatic replay workers. Legacy charged-job refunds remain until charged jobs and refund debt are drained or migrated.
- Error tracking (Sentry), product analytics (PostHog, Firebase Analytics), web-vitals reporting, metrics sinks and speculative span-label pre-warming. Logs are Pino on the server and the console in the browser; see [observability](docs/architecture/observability.md).
- Screens and presentations: draft comparison, management and discard screens; context or coupled-setting approval gates; technical attachment, storage and renderer repair screens; billing, checkout and credit receipts; extra retry confirmation; the three-choice "This draft changed elsewhere" modal; routine Saved/Saving and replacement-success labels; "Saved; add to conversation" notices; "What are you making?" headings; the composer action chooser.
- Unfinished work is reached through conversation history. Retry is a direct action with a new execution identity; uncertain acceptance still reconciles the original identity. Conflict detection, retained-copy protection, data retention, media ownership, idempotency and internal recovery remain required.
