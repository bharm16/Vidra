# Competitive workflows and implications for Vidra

Research date and source access date: **2026-10-09, America/Chicago**.

Scope: Higgsfield, Runway and Krea, using their public product pages and documentation. This records advertised behavior, not hands-on acceptance testing, comparative output quality, customer demand or demonstrated profitability. No accounts were created, generations purchased or provider calls made. Recommendations below are hypotheses, not adopted product decisions.

## What this changes for Vidra

**Guided authoring is already contested.** The historical claim in [ADR-0002](../adr/0002-vidra-is-an-authoring-tool-for-non-experts.md) that the labs are not building authoring is no longer a sound competitive premise. Higgsfield documents reviewable AI direction; Runway and Krea advertise conversational creation and revisions. Better wording, model access, a chat panel, a drawing surface or a connected canvas alone do not establish a reason to switch. This is an inference from the cited product documentation, not a claim that those competitors execute every promise well.

**All three desired outputs can belong to one product.** Stills, usable shots and finished social videos need a common project and assets, with different completion requirements. They do not need a common mandatory sequence. An image can be finished without becoming a video; a video can start from text or an uploaded clip; an existing selection of clips can go directly into an edit. Studio and Sketch should help someone do a particular task without creating competing ownership of the project.

**A generated clip is not necessarily a finished social video.** Vidra must make the promise explicit: a shot can be delivered as a downloadable source asset; a finished video may also need ordering, trimming, timing, audio, captions, titles and format-specific exports. These are product requirements to choose and implement, not things automatically provided by successful generation. Competitors explicitly market assembly and multi-format delivery, so labeling every completed generation “finished” would weaken Vidra's claim. [Krea Agent](https://www.krea.ai/docs/user-guide/agent/overview), [Runway Agent](https://runway.com/product/agent)

**Revision and recovery are part of the paid product.** A project can contain a usable still, a failed shot attempt, a running alternative and an export that needs repair simultaneously. Its state cannot be represented truthfully by one global position in an idea-to-video funnel. Each operation needs its own input snapshot, status and recovery action; each result needs its own selection and delivery status. The cited competitor recovery pages support treating failures as ordinary operation states. They do not prove which competitor has better persistence or recovery.

## Two concrete product hypotheses

These are alternative ways to frame the same three outputs, not claims of empty market space. Neither requires customers to follow a prescribed creation order.

| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A project workspace for solo creators and freelance editors**       | Someone building a social project can generate or import stills and shots, revise selected material, and assemble a short finished video in the same project. Source images, individual shots and finished exports are independently useful deliverables. | Accepted versions remain identifiable; one change does not silently replace another accepted result; reusable source assets and final exports can be reopened and downloaded; unresolved jobs remain visible without blocking unrelated work. | Competes with Runway's projects and tools, Krea's editing and Agent, and Higgsfield's studios. It must demonstrate less work to make a requested revision and finish delivery, not merely fewer buttons.                   |
| **A content workspace for one small business's recurring promotions** | A business or its contracted creator works with its product photos, approved copy and brand materials to produce still variations, useful video shots and finished short promotions. Start from whichever materials already exist.                        | Deliver a named set of correctly sized assets with editable copy/layout and explicit versions. Show which source materials were preserved directly and which were regenerated; keep factual product/copy review with the creator.             | Competes directly with Higgsfield Marketing Studio, Runway Agent and Krea Agent. It must earn preference through dependable revisions and repeat work for the same business. “AI ads” by itself offers no differentiation. |

The stronger near-term engineering commonality is the same for both: projects, selected assets, local edits, independent jobs and explicit exports. Implement dependencies in that foundation without forcing them into the user's journey. If the eventual public offer promises all three outputs, its release acceptance must exercise all three; finishing stills and shots alone does not fulfill the finished-video promise.

## Revenue implications

[ADR-0010](../adr/0010-one-visible-text-one-loop-subscription-at-keep.md) proposed free rerolls and a subscription at Keep. [ADR-0023](../adr/0023-bounded-free-validation-proposal.md) intentionally supersedes payment for supervised free testing and leaves the paid/public offering open. Preserve that historical distinction. None of the inspected competitor material justifies Vidra promising unlimited free attempts, a particular price or revenue from export gating.

A plausible paid offer is project creation and revision with a clearly bounded generation allowance, plus additional usage when requested. The UI need not copy competitors' credit terminology, but the product must expose cost before a charge and have an enforceable spending limit. Downloading an already accepted asset should not secretly trigger a new generative attempt whose result may differ. Upscaling, generation and ordinary file export are distinct operations.

Price only after measuring the provider, language-model, retry, export and storage cost required to complete each kind of deliverable. Track accepted deliveries and revision effort alongside raw generation success. Failed technical attempts, unwanted but successfully generated results, and failed file attachment/export need separate policies. Competitors' pricing structures show that these costs exist; they do not reveal Vidra's unit economics or establish willingness to pay.

## Evidence limits

- No live competitor accounts, paid checkout or creative-quality comparisons were exercised.
- No exact price recommendation is made. Plan toggles, promotions, model access and allowances change; consult the linked official pages before a purchase or public comparison.
- Model names and broad duration claims in documentation can lag actual offers. This research uses the documented interaction patterns; it does not qualify a current provider/model compatibility matrix.
- The hypotheses require completion and cost evidence before revenue claims. They are not authorization for paid provider tests, a billing rollout or a public launch.
